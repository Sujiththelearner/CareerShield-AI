The "Laptop Courier Deposit" Trap

Fraudsters send convincing fake offer letters from big brands (Google, Amazon, TCS) and ask the student to transfer ₹2,500 - ₹5,000 as a "courier deposit" for an enterprise laptop or ID badge. Remember: Real companies absorb all shipping costs. Never send advance money!
